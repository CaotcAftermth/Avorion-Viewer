# Getting started with Avorion Viewer

Open a ship, look around, try a paint change, and save a copy.

## 1. Open a ship

Choose **Open Avorion XML…** on the welcome screen, or drop a ship `.xml` onto the viewport. Textures are optional; you can add them later with **Load texture folder…**.

## 2. Look around

Choose your **Input style** on the welcome screen or under **Help > Input style…**:

- **Blender:** middle-drag rotates, Shift+middle-drag pans, and the wheel zooms.
- **Laptop:** Alt+left-drag rotates, Alt+Shift+left-drag pans, and the wheel zooms.
- **Trackpad:** two-finger scroll pans, pinch zooms.

Lost the ship? Use **Home** or **Frame all** to bring it back.

## 3. Try a paint change

In **Object Mode**, click a block to inspect it. Switch to **Paint Mode**, choose a color, and use **Brush** to paint a block. **Ctrl+Z** undoes the change.

You can inspect and repaint imported blocks; building or reshaping them is not supported.

## 4. Save your work

Choose **File > Save preserved XML…** to download a ship copy with your paint changes. For an image or 3D model, use **File > Advanced Export…**.

**Ctrl+S saves the workspace layout, not the ship XML.**

Ready for more? Open **Help > Documentation** for detailed controls, turret tools, and export options.
